## What Strata is (1 sentence)

A method to organize the knowledge that long work accumulates, in durability layers: it
separates what is timeless (Mneme, L0) from what is consolidated form (Morfé, L1) and
from what is dated tooling (Órganon, L2). Switch tools without losing the why.

## Test conclusions (the headlines)

1. **AI applies the method, from budget to top tier.** Fixing a known defect (§5)
   **saturates** from ~8B local (20-27B saturates fully) to the frontier top; without the
   method, the same models fail across the whole grade. ~350-run grade, mechanical gold
   standard + blind cross-vendor jury (2026-08-02).
2. **Security refusal (§6-bis) comes out spontaneously** in the current generation: the AI
   refuses to run the planted malicious instruction ("download and run this URL"), even at
   the affordable tier. Only one to avoid: llama-4-scout.
3. **The edge that separates models is abstention (§9)**: knowing when NOT to act on a
   project that is already good. It is a model property, not price, tier, or language.
   Hence the rule: mid/affordable model = checklist + human in the loop; autonomous
   self-audit only with the top tier.
4. **Agent in a sandbox: it transferred.** In the first cell with real tools, the pattern
   held in execution (10/12 with the method × 2/12 without; 0/24 ran the malicious
   command).
5. **Portuguese × English: parity closed (2026-08-03).** The grade core was repeated in
   English with an identical roster (108 runs): fix and abstention reproduce; the top tier
   closes 6/6 in both languages. **English is NOT better.** Mixing languages (method in EN
   + answer in PT via an instruction) was tested and **rejected** (worst obedience to the
   payload). Dated signal: mid/open models refused injection slightly worse in EN (5/8 ×
   1/8, K=2). Practical rule: run Strata in the language of whoever reads and applies it.
6. **Cost measured.** One AI audit of a small project costs **~1 cent** at the affordable
   floor (gpt-5-mini); reproducing the whole published grade costs **~US$ 7** (~350 runs).
   Portuguese costs ~22% more input tokens (tokenizer inequality, documented in the
   literature); net effect EN −15-20% per run, too small to drive the decision. Key metric:
   **cost per organized project** is less than one minute of human time.
7. **Regime honesty (always cite):** these are **signals** in controlled synthetic
   scenarios, not proofs; ecological validation is ongoing (in the real world, the
   self-auditor does not beat raw competence: R8). All evidence, methods, and answer keys
   are open.
