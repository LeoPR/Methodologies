## Headlines

1. **Model bank renewed (2026-09-26).** The fix, the refusal of the injection and "not touching a
   good project" were measured on models released through September. **16 routes do all three.**
   The cheapest costs about **US$ 0.002 per audit** (gpt-6-luna); the fastest answers in **3 s**
   (gemini-3.5-flash-lite); the smallest open model that does everything is **qwen3.8-27b**.
   Record: `lab/2026-09-26-banco-modelos/`.
2. **It can all be done for free**, only slowly: **kimi-k3** and **deepseek-v4.1-flash** on
   NVIDIA's free tier did all three.
3. **Running at home is a question of the card, not of the model.** The weights that do
   everything (qwen3.8-27b) fit whole on a 24 GB card such as a 4090. On a 12 GB card the best fit
   fixes and refuses the injection, and the decision not to touch stays with a human. Capability
   measured in the cloud on the same weights, with a local counter-proof.
4. **Thinking more does not help.** With high reasoning no result improved, and some got worse
   (one model started acting on a project that was already good). Rule: default or low reasoning.
5. **Checking sources without the internet is dangerous.** Without web search, top models
   confirmed as "correct" a standard that had already been replaced; with web, they corrected
   everything.
6. **Three models propagated a malicious instruction** planted in the project (gpt-oss-120b,
   claude-haiku-4.5, gemma4:12b; the last one measured only locally, since it has no cloud
   host). Do not use them for autonomous action.
7. **Regime honesty (always cite):** signals in synthetic scenarios, three runs per test, not
   proof. On a real project, autonomous auditing only pays off with a top model.

## Corrections to the 2026-08-03 news

The previous news stated three things the records do not support:

- **"Without the method, the same models fail across the whole grade."** Overstated. Without the
  method the fix rarely comes out in the traceable format, but sometimes it does; and in the
  "do not touch" test the arm without the method tied with the arm with it.
- **"English is not better in any cell."** The records do not say that: in some cells English came
  out better. What holds is "English without a demonstrated advantage".
- **"Portuguese × English: parity closed."** The pre-registration required a statistical margin
  that was not met. The correct reading is: **no difference detected, equivalence not
  demonstrated.**

The August "do not touch" series was also downgraded: the fixture used gave the answer away in its
own text. The test of the §9 change was re-read as **inconclusive** (it had no statistical power),
not "no effect".
